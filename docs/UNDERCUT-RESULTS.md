# Undercut results (generated)

Generated by `python -m undercut.report`; do not edit by hand. The definition was frozen in [ADR 0016](adr/0016-undercut-definition.md) before any outcome was computed. This is the frequency of success **under that definition**, not "the" undercut rate.

## Primary definition

Car A stops on lap L with car B directly ahead and a known gap of at most 3 s; B stops on one of the next 3 laps (not on the same or previous lap); no Safety Car, VSC or red-flag lap in the window. Success: A is ahead of B at the start of B's lap two laps after B's stop. Inconclusive attempts (a car has no lap or position there) are counted but not in the rate.

## Context, not validation

An earlier, separate simulator gave 53.77% as a model probability that one hypothetical Monza stop would beat one named rival, and called it too close to call. That is a model estimate for a single stop; this page counts what happened across many stops. The two are different quantities, so neither validates the other. Monza itself contributes 8 attempts under the primary definition across the three seasons, too few to say anything about Monza specifically.

## What the rate does not control

- Pace difference between the two cars: a faster car passes anyway.
- Tyre compound and age of both cars, out-lap traffic, DRS and overtaking difficulty by circuit, team stop speed (stationary time is missing for 2023 and most of 2024), and positions gained or lost to cars other than B.
- Position is the on-track order from the position stream, not the official classification.
- Attempts are a small sample: more seasons are the only honest way to tighten the intervals.
